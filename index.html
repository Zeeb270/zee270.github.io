About Me

A brief introduction to who I am and what drives my research.

I am an applied mathematician specializing in optimization, operational research, and data-driven modelling, with a strong focus on scheduling under uncertainty and intelligent decision systems.

I completed the prestigious Erasmus Mundus RealMaths Double Degree programme at the University of L'Aquila (Italy) and the University of Aveiro (Portugal).

My master's thesis developed a hybrid Fuzzy-ACO framework for adaptive scheduling of multi-product pipelines. I currently work as a Research Fellow at CIDMA, University of Aveiro, focusing on global overfitting, forecasting methods, adaptive ensemble learning, and data-driven optimization for predictive decision systems.
